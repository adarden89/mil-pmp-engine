◎

Make your rings

Five ways to do it together in San Diego, from welded gold chains to solid gold bands you make in a day. Do it before New Year's Eve so you're both wearing them at midnight. Pick one and choose the day; it counts toward the season budget.

Which metal? Sterling silver darkens over time and polishes back in a minute. Gold-filled is a thick layer of real gold bonded over brass; with care it lasts years, but it can't be refinished like solid gold. Solid gold is forever and can be resized later. A permanent chain isn't a ring at all: it's welded on, and you snip it off if you ever need to.

Notes, assumptions & sources

Every date, price, address and link on this page was checked on Nov 13, 2026. Each card's "Checked" line says where. Most were confirmed on the organizer's own website; where that page wouldn't load or didn't show a price, I used current listings and said so. Anything labeled "estimate" is mine. Travel times from Park 12 are my estimates.

Corrections from that check: Elf ends Jan 3, not Jan 4. The Nutcracker has no show on Dec 21. Whale watching starts Dec 12. The 2026 New Year's Eve cruise is $163.77 a person. The Del's 2026 party is a "Rockin' New Year's Eve" at The Laundry, not a 1920s speakeasy. METHRA is $120 a person. Lightscape parking is $15. The gondola sunset cruise is $164 for two, and you may bring wine for a corkage fee. Two links were dead (the Del's dining page and a past candle class) and were replaced. I removed the salsa festival, because its passes cost $150–$250 each and its dates conflict between listings.

Not confirmed: whether San Diego has public fireworks at midnight; current prices for the Grinch, Spa Estancia, Whaley Studios, San Diego Jewelry Academy, permanent chains and ember&onyx's Dec 5 class; Christmas Card Lane's exact dates. Whaley Studios' website didn't load when I checked. The zoo's military policy comes from military.com and NBC San Diego, not the zoo's own site. Resy credits only work at restaurants marked eligible (since Aug 1, 2026).
