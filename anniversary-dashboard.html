Notes, assumptions & sources

Hotel rates, dinner costs and a few ticket prices are my estimates (typical rate plus the holiday premium, with tax). Check live prices before booking. Every Amex credit needs enrollment, and the restaurant must show the Amex badge on Resy.

Credit rules used: Platinum hotel credit $300 Jul–Dec 2026 and $300 Jan–Jun 2027, for prepaid FHR or THC (THC needs 2 nights; from Jan 1, 2027 FHR also needs 2 nights). Each hotel earns its own $100 property credit; back-to-back bookings at the same hotel would count as one. Resy: Gold $50 per half-year, Platinum $100 per quarter. Uber Cash: $35 Platinum + $10 Gold in December, $25 in January. The Saks credit ended June 30, 2026, and the airline credit doesn't apply because this weekend has no flights.

Military: Surfliner 15% (code V780, active duty and spouses; veterans also 15% in California), $5 off Skating by the Sea, USS Midway free for active duty, Pandora 10% via ID.me (not 14k gold). "Andrew's own Platinum" assumes she's active duty and Amex waives the fee under the Military Lending Act; confirm with Amex.

Left out on purpose: Disneyland (the most crowded weekend of the year) and Pasadena hotels (Rose Parade prices).
