A · Gold ring-making workshops

Gold-filled or solid 14k, for two

14k Gold-Filled

$90–$178 / person

A thick layer of 14k gold bonded to a brass core, at least 5% gold by weight. It holds its color for years with care, but it isn't solid gold and can't really be resized.

For two: ~$180–$356 · I use $150/pp

Solid 14k Yellow Gold

$350–$650+ / person

58.5% gold all the way through, so it can be resized and passed down. Spot gold was about $4,050/oz in early November 2026, down from its $5,608 peak in January. That puts 14k at about $76/g at melt, and a 2 mm band uses 2.5–4 g, so metal alone is $190–$300 per ring before the studio's markup.

For two: ~$700–$1,300 · I use $500/pp · ask for per-gram pricing

The Jan 1 reset play

8/10

Your trip crosses into a new credit period. Book Dec 31 now as its own 1-night FHR stay, which uses the H2 2026 $300. Then, on the morning of Jan 1, book Jan 1–3 as a separate 2-night FHR stay, which uses the H1 2027 $300. That's two $100 property credits and breakfast both days. Platinum Resy ($100 for Q1), Gold Resy ($50 for H1) and January Uber Cash ($25) also reset at midnight for your Jan 1 or 2 dinner.

  • Risks: rates and availability on Jan 1, a possible room change (ask the hotel to link the stays), and Amex reading the charge date (book the second stay after midnight Eastern to be safe).
  • Protect yourself by holding a refundable direct booking for Jan 1–3 as a backup, then cancelling it once the FHR booking goes through.
  • Using the January credits now means they won't cover a meal later in Q1. The calculator keeps them off by default for that reason.
